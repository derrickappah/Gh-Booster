What is Partial Status in Orders?

Partial Status is when we partially refund the remaining quantity of an order. Sometimes for technical reasons a service cannot deliver 100% of the quantity. If an order becomes Partial, the undelivered quantity's cost is automatically refunded back to your panel balance.

How does the 30-Day Refill Button work?

If you order a service labeled with [30 Days Refill] and your follower/like count drops, go to Orders History and click the Refill button. Our automated server will check the link and refill the dropped count within 24 hours.

How do Mobile Money (MTN / Telecel / AT) deposits work in Ghana?

Go to Add Funds, enter your GHS deposit amount, select your Mobile Money provider, and type your MoMo number. You will receive an instant prompt on your phone to approve the PIN!

What is Drip-Feed and how do I use it?

Drip-Feed allows you to deliver an order in small batches over multiple runs. For example: If you want 10,000 Instagram followers delivered as 1,000 followers every day for 10 days, set Quantity = 1000, Runs = 10, Interval = 1440 (minutes in a day).

How do I connect my website via API?

Check out our comprehensive API Documentation. You can fetch your API key from Account Settings and connect any SMM website or script to resell GhBooster services seamlessly.
