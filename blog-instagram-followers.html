1. Understanding the Instagram Recommendation Engine

Instagram’s algorithm operates on a feedback loop of engagement metrics. Before recommending your post on the Explore page or Reels tab, it analyzes the early signals of your content. Specifically, it tracks:

  • Watch Time & Retention: Do users scroll past, or do they pause and watch your Reels fully?
  • Interaction Rate: Likes, comments, shares, and saves relative to your current follower size.
  • Account Authority: The historical credibility of your account, which is heavily influenced by your base size and consistency.
