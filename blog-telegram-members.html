1. The Psychology of Telegram Channels

Unlike search-engine based networks, Telegram is a closed community ecosystem. When a user is shared a link to your Telegram channel, they make an onboarding decision in less than two seconds.

They check two key signals:

  • Total Member Count: Does this community look established, or does it look like a dead project?
