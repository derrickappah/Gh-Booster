Finding a reliable SMM (Social Media Marketing) panel in West Africa has historically been plagued by payment gateway friction and high follower drop rates. This audit tests GhBooster's speed, payment infrastructure, and safety guarantees.

1. The Payment Gateway: Ghana Mobile Money Integration

The biggest friction point for SMM resellers in Ghana has been international card payments, which trigger bank blocks or charge heavy conversion fees.

GhBooster resolves this by integrating direct, automated local **Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AT)** gateways.

We tested order delivery times across the core social media platforms:

We audited the retention rate of GhBooster's non-drop follower services over a 30-day monitoring window.

The server validated the request instantly and automatically delivered 50 fresh followers within 8 hours to restore the count.
